The story

Not a conventional project manager. A builder becoming more intentional with every project.

Uwemabasi’s professional story is evolving around a simple strength: she sees the human side of execution. She understands that projects move when communication is clear, responsibilities are visible, people feel supported and someone keeps the whole picture connected.

Her earlier work across customer-facing and administrative environments developed the discipline behind that approach: listening carefully, coordinating under pressure, keeping records accurate, following through and helping people move from confusion to clarity. Today, those strengths are being channelled into project and operations leadership at ONE Light Analytics.

She is building toward a career that sits at the intersection of project management, operational leadership and social impact, with a particular interest in humanitarian work, vulnerable populations, community wellbeing and people whose needs are too easily overlooked.
